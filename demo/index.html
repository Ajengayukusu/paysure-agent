6 · Try it live — write on-chain with your own wallet

Everything above is read-only and needs no wallet. This panel writes on-chain through your Web3 wallet (EIP-1193, e.g. MetaMask): the page builds the calldata locally and your wallet signs — this page never holds any key. Roles in one line: the payee registers its name · any merchant creates invoices · only the wallet holding the owner’s agent session can pay them, inside its caps.
